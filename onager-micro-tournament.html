RULES & GAME FORMAT

Every player controls 3 Siege Onagers, in an arena style map. Units are in close quarters, so there's no where to hide.

01

3 Qualifier Rounds

All 8 players enter the arena across 3 rounds in a free-for-all. The last player alive in each round claims 1 round point.

02

Most Kills Advancement

A finalist spot is awarded to the player with the highest overall kills across Rounds 1–3 as well.

03

Finals Twist: Villager

Each player with a round point and the top kills face off. In Round 4, each player spawns with 3 Siege Onagers + 1 Repair Villager.
